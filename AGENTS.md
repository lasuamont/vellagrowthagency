# Vella Growth - Brand & Development Guidelines

## Overview

This document provides comprehensive brand and development guidelines for Vella Growth, a specialist digital marketing agency for destination management companies (DMCs) and concierge travel companies targeting the US travel market.

**Primary Goal:** Generate requests for free visibility audits through a polished, responsive single-page website.

---

## 1. Brand Positioning & Target Audience

### Who We Are
Vella Growth is a boutique digital marketing agency specializing in destination management companies (DMCs) and concierge travel companies.

### Positioning Statement
The growth partner that understands the travel trade, so travel companies get found in search and in AI answers.

### Promise
More qualified enquiries and more direct bookings, with less dependence on resellers.

### Target Audiences
- **Boutique DMC owners and managing directors**: Want more direct bookings and a credible online presence. Pain points include dependence on resellers, outdated websites, and no time for marketing.
- **Marketing/digital managers at mid-sized DMCs and concierge travel companies**: Need to grow organic traffic and show up in AI assistants. Pain points include scattered data, difficulty proving ROI, and limited travel-specific SEO knowledge.

### Personality
- **Calm**: Never loud or pushy
- **Precise**: Clear, practical, short sentences, plain English
- **Premium**: Refined and warm, never flashy
- **Trustworthy**: Honest, transparent, results-focused

## 3. Tone of Voice Principles

### Do
- ✅ Speak travel: use terms like itineraries, partners, bookings, DMC
- ✅ Explain GEO simply: "being recommended by AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Gemini"
- ✅ Lead with benefit, then explain method
- ✅ Use short sentences and plain English
- ✅ Use real numbers instead of adjectives
- ✅ Be calm, assured, and professional

### Avoid
- ❌ Hype words: revolutionary, skyrocket, game-changing
- ❌ Exclamation marks and all-caps shouting
- ❌ Jargon without explanation
- ❌ Generic AI-sounding marketing copy
- ❌ Buzzwords and exaggerated claims
- ❌ Excessive adjectives

### Microcopy Examples

| Context | Use | Not |
|---------|-----|-----|
| Button | "Book a free visibility audit" | "Get started now!" |
| Button | "See how we work" | "Learn more" |
| Form note | "We reply within one business day." | "We'll get back to you soon!" |
| Error | "Something went wrong. Please try again." | "Oops! Try again!" |

## 4. Layout & Component Rules

### Grid System
- **Columns**: 12-column desktop grid
- **Max Width**: 1200px (7xl in Tailwind)
- **Gutters**: 24px desktop, 16px mobile
- **Side Margins**: 24px (6 in Tailwind)

### Breakpoints
- **Mobile**: up to 640px
- **Tablet**: 641px to 1024px
- **Desktop**: 1025px and up
- **Design Approach**: Mobile-first

### Spacing
- **Base Unit**: 8px increments
- **Scale**: 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128px
- **Section Padding**: 96px desktop, 64px mobile
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Use consistent spacing between sections

### Border Radius
- **Cards**: 16px
- **Buttons & Inputs**: 12px

### Shadows
- **Soft Shadow**: 0 8px 30px rgba(18, 27, 34, 0.08)
- **Usage**: Use sparingly. Prefer borders and color changes over shadows.

### Focus States
- **Focus Ring**: 2px solid #C2AC92 (champagne)
- **Focus Offset**: 2px
- **Apply to**: All interactive elements

### Borders
- **Default**: 1px solid #EBE7E4 (stone)
- **Usage**: Subtle, restrained

## 5. Visual Style

### Imagery
- ✅ Real destination photography with warm, natural light
- ✅ Slightly muted colors to complement navy and cream
- ✅ Wide crops with generous empty space for text
- ✅ Add navy overlay (40-60%) when text sits on photos
- ❌ Avoid generic stock images
- ❌ Avoid staged handshakes or generic office teams

### Icons & Graphics
- ✅ Thin line icons (1.5px stroke)
- ✅ Rounded ends on strokes
- ✅ Navy or silver colors
- ✅ Single champagne dot as accent, echoing the logo
- ✅ Use silver gradient for large graphic shapes
- ✅ Keep illustrations minimal and abstract
- ❌ Avoid heavy, complex icons

### Motion
- ✅ Subtle fade and rise animations on scroll (300-500ms, ease-out)
- ✅ Hover changes in 200ms
- ✅ Button fills champagne on hover
- ✅ Links gain underline on hover
- ❌ No heavy parallax effects
- ❌ No auto-playing carousels
- ❌ No unnecessary animations
- ⚠️ **Must respect**: `prefers-reduced-motion` setting

### Transitions
- **Fade In**: 500ms ease-out
- **Rise Up**: 500ms ease-out (from 20px below)
- **Hover**: 200ms

## 7. Accessibility Requirements

### Must Have
- ✅ Text contrast minimum 4.5:1 for normal text
- ✅ NEVER use champagne text on cream backgrounds (2.1:1 contrast - fails)
- ✅ Visible focus ring on all interactive elements
- ✅ Full keyboard navigation support
- ✅ Meaningful alt text on all images
- ✅ Proper labels on all form fields
- ✅ Semantic HTML (use correct heading hierarchy)
- ✅ Minimum 44px touch targets for mobile
- ✅ Reduced-motion support via `@media (prefers-reduced-motion)`
- ✅ ARIA attributes where appropriate
- ✅ Skip to content link (consider adding)

### Color Contrast Ratios
| Combination | Ratio | Status |
|-------------|-------|--------|
| Ink Navy on Cream | 16.6:1 | ✅ Pass AA |
| Cream on Ink Navy | 16.6:1 | ✅ Pass AA |
| Bronze on Cream | 5.0:1 | ✅ Pass AA |
| Steel Silver on Cream | 3.2:1 | ⚠️ Large text only |
| Champagne on Cream | 2.1:1 | ❌ Do NOT use for text |
| Champagne on Ink Navy | 8.0:1 | ✅ Pass AA |

## 8. SEO & GEO Requirements

### Metadata
- **Page Title**: Under 60 characters
- **Meta Description**: Under 155 characters
- **H1**: One per page
- **Heading Hierarchy**: H1 → H2 → H3 (logical structure)

### Structured Data
- ✅ Organization schema
- ✅ Service schema
- ✅ FAQPage schema (for FAQ section)
- ❌ NEVER add schema claims not supported by page content

### Open Graph
- ✅ Title, description, URL
- ✅ Site name: "Vella Growth"
- ✅ Locale: en_US
- ✅ Type: website
- ✅ Images: 1200x630px

### Technical SEO
- ✅ Clean, descriptive URLs
- ✅ XML sitemap
- ✅ robots.txt
- ✅ Canonical URLs
- ✅ Proper link relationships

### GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
- ✅ FAQ blocks with short, direct answers AI can quote
- ✅ Clear, structured content
- ✅ Content that answers specific travel-related questions
- ✅ Avoid vague or marketing-heavy language

## 9. Performance Requirements

### Core Web Vitals
- ✅ Largest Contentful Paint: < 2.5 seconds
- ✅ First Input Delay: < 100ms
- ✅ Cumulative Layout Shift: < 0.1

### Optimization
- ✅ Images in WebP or AVIF format
- ✅ Lazy loading for below-the-fold media
- ✅ Proper image dimensions and alt text
- ✅ Font loading with `display: swap`
- ✅ Minimal JavaScript
- ✅ Code splitting where appropriate
- ✅ Avoid render-blocking resources

### Font Loading
- **Max 2 font families** (Cormorant Garamond, Montserrat)
- **Max 4 weights total**
- **Preload** critical fonts

## 10. Content Rules - PROHIBITIONS

### ❌ NEVER Invent or Fabricate

**Client Information:**
- ❌ Client logos (unless verified and real)
- ❌ Client names
- ❌ Testimonials
- ❌ Case study data
- ❌ Performance percentages or metrics
- ❌ Client quotes

**Team Information:**
- ❌ Founder names
- ❌ Personal histories
- ❌ Biographies
- ❌ Photographs
- ❌ Team member details

**Business Claims:**
- ❌ Guaranteed search rankings
- ❌ Guaranteed AI recommendations
- ❌ Specific business results (unless verified)
- ❌ Readership statistics
- ❌ Research findings
- ❌ Invented metrics

### ✅ What to Do Instead

**For Client Logos/Trust:**
- Use simple editorial text: "Serving: Destination Management Companies · Concierge Travel · Destination Specialists"
- Leave placeholders that are easy to replace with real logos later

**For Case Studies:**
- Design sections to show approach/methodology
- Use service examples labeled as "illustrative" or "example"
- Make cards easy to replace with real case studies
- State: "Results are measured against each client's starting point and business objectives"

**For Team:**
- Focus on Vella's approach and methodology
- Use generic descriptions: "Our team of travel specialists..."
- Leave pages easy to update with real information later

**For Metrics:**
- Only use verified data from the repository
- If no verified data exists, describe the measurement approach instead

## 11. Conversion Strategy

### Primary Goal
Generate requests for **free visibility audits**

### Conversion Path
1. Hero section → "Book a Free Audit" CTA
2. Mid-page (Services/Why Vella) → "See How We Work" / "Explore Services" CTAs
3. Closing section (Contact) → "Request Your Audit" form

### Form Requirements
- **Fields**: Name, Work Email, Company, Website, GDPR Consent
- **Max Fields**: 4 (plus consent)
- **Reply Promise**: "We reply within one business day."
- **Validation**: Clear, helpful error messages
- **Submission**: Clear loading and success states
- **Backend**: Use configured form backend if available, otherwise provide fallback

### CTA Language
- Primary: "Book a Free Visibility Audit" / "Request Your Audit"
- Secondary: "See How We Work" / "Explore Our Services" / "Discuss Your Visibility"
- Form Submit: "REQUEST YOUR AUDIT"

## 13. Testing Requirements

### Before Deployment

1. **Build Test**
   ```bash
   npm run build
   ```
   - ✅ Must complete without errors
   - ✅ Must generate production-ready output

2. **Lint Test**
   ```bash
   npm run lint
   ```
   - ✅ Must pass without errors
   - ⚠️ Warnings should be addressed

3. **Type Check**
   ```bash
   npm run type-check  # or npx tsc --noEmit
   ```
   - ✅ Must pass without errors

4. **Responsive Testing**
   - ✅ Test on mobile (320px - 640px)
   - ✅ Test on tablet (641px - 1024px)
   - ✅ Test on desktop (1025px+)
   - ✅ Verify all layouts at all breakpoints

5. **Accessibility Testing**
   - ✅ Keyboard navigation (Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter)
   - ✅ Screen reader testing (VoiceOver/NVDA)
   - ✅ Color contrast verification
   - ✅ Focus management
   - ✅ Form validation

6. **Link Testing**
   - ✅ All internal links work
   - ✅ All anchor links scroll to correct sections
   - ✅ External links open in new tabs (if applicable)
   - ✅ No broken links

7. **Form Testing**
   - ✅ Validation works correctly
   - ✅ Error messages display properly
   - ✅ Submission states work
   - ✅ Success/error states display
   - ✅ Form is accessible

8. **Performance Testing**
   - ✅ Lighthouse score > 90 (Desktop)
   - ✅ Lighthouse score > 80 (Mobile)
   - ✅ LCP < 2.5s
   - ✅ No performance warnings in console

## 15. Maintenance Guidelines

### Updating Content
1. Replace placeholder text with real content
2. Add real client logos when available
3. Add verified case studies when available
4. Update team information when available
5. Always follow brand tone of voice

### Adding New Pages
1. Follow existing component patterns
2. Use established color and typography
3. Maintain consistent spacing
4. Add proper metadata
5. Ensure accessibility
6. Test responsive layouts

### Updating Styles
1. Use existing Tailwind classes where possible
2. Add new colors to theme only if absolutely necessary
3. Maintain color balance (60/30/10)
4. Preserve typography hierarchy
5. Test contrast ratios for new color combinations
